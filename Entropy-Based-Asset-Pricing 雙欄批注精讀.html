Abstract ｜ 摘要

¶1We investigate entropy as a financial risk measure. Entropy explains the equity premium of securities and portfolios in a simpler way and, at the same time, with higher explanatory power than the beta parameter of the capital asset pricing model. For asset pricing we define the continuous entropy as an alternative measure of risk. Our results show that entropy decreases in the function of the number of securities involved in a portfolio in a similar way to the standard deviation, and that efficient portfolios are situated on a hyperbola in the expected return – entropy system. For empirical investigation we use daily returns of 150 randomly selected securities for a period of 27 years. Our regression results show that entropy has a higher explanatory power for the expected return than the capital asset pricing model beta. Furthermore we show the time varying behavior of the beta along with entropy.

本文將熵視為一種金融風險度量進行考察。相較於資本資產定價模型（CAPM）中的貝塔係數，熵不僅能以更簡潔的方式解釋個別證券與投資組合的股權溢酬，其解釋力亦更高。我們定義連續熵作為替代性風險度量，用於資產定價。結果顯示：熵隨投資組合內證券數量增加而遞減，行為與標準差相似；且在「期望報酬－熵」座標系中，效率投資組合落在一條雙曲線上。實證部分採用 150 檔隨機抽取證券、橫跨 27 年的日報酬資料。迴歸結果顯示，熵對期望報酬的解釋力高於 CAPM 貝塔；此外，本文也呈現了貝塔與熵隨時間變動的行為。

Introduction ｜ 引言

¶2We build an equilibrium capital asset pricing model by applying a novel risk measure, the entropy. Entropy characterizes the uncertainty or measures the dispersion of a random variable. In our particular case, it characterizes the uncertainty of stock and portfolio returns. In modern Markowitz portfolio theory and equilibrium asset pricing models we apply linear regressions. This methodology supposes that the returns are stationary and normally distributed; however, this is not actually the case. Entropy, on the other hand, does not have this kind of boundary condition. The main goal of this paper is to apply entropy as a novel risk measure. As a starting point even the density function itself has to be estimated. In the traditional asset pricing model there is equilibrium between expected return and the beta parameter, which is the covariance–variance ratio between the market portfolio and the investigated investment opportunity. If the random variable is normally distributed then the entropy follows its standard deviation; thus in the ideal case there is no difference between the two risk measures. However, our results show that there is a significant difference between the standard deviation, or beta, and the entropy of a given security or portfolio.

本文以一種新穎的風險度量——熵——構建均衡資產定價模型。熵刻畫隨機變數的不確定性或離散程度；在本文脈絡下，即刻畫股票與投資組合報酬的不確定性。Markowitz 組合理論與均衡定價模型依賴線性迴歸，此方法隱含報酬「平穩且常態分布」的假設，但實際數據並不符合。熵則不受此類邊界條件約束。本文的主要目標即是將熵應用為新的風險度量；而其起點，是連密度函數本身都必須先行估計。傳統定價模型中，期望報酬與貝塔（即標的與市場組合之間的共變異數—變異數比值）處於均衡關係。若隨機變數服從常態分布，熵將與標準差同步變動，理想情況下兩種風險度量並無差異；然而本文結果顯示，對給定證券或組合而言，標準差（或貝塔）與熵之間存在顯著差異。

¶3In this paper we show that entropy offers an ideal alternative for capturing the risk of an investment opportunity. If we explain the return of a wide sample of securities and portfolios with different risk measures then on an ordinary least squares (OLS) regression setting the explanatory power is much higher in the case of the entropy measure of risk than in the case of the traditional measures, both in-sample and out-of-sample. We show that entropy reduction in line with diversification behaves similarly to standard deviation; however at the same time it captures a beta-like systematic risk of single securities or non-efficient portfolios as well. For well-diversified portfolios the explanatory power of entropy is 1.5 times higher than that of the capital asset pricing model (CAPM) beta.

本文主張，熵是捕捉投資機會風險的理想替代方案。若以不同風險度量解釋大樣本證券與投資組合的報酬，在 OLS 迴歸架構下，無論樣本內或樣本外，熵的解釋力都遠高於傳統度量。我們證明：熵隨分散化遞減的行為與標準差相似，但同時又能像貝塔一樣捕捉單一證券或非效率組合的系統性風險。對充分分散的投資組合而言，熵的解釋力是 CAPM 貝塔的 1.5 倍。

¶4We also test and compare entropy with standard risk measures for market circumstances that are increasing and decreasing, and find that the explanatory power of entropy is significantly higher in a bullish market, but lower for a bearish market. Our results for bullish and bearish regimes show that the different risk measures behave similarly in terms of the positive and negative relationship between risk and return. This behavior underlines the fact that the entropy-based risk measure can give contradictory results in the same way as traditional risk estimations in upward and downward regimes. We also compare the entropy-based risk measures with the CAPM beta in and out of sample, which gives information on the predictive power of the different methods. As the CAPM beta measures the systematic risk only, while entropy-based risk measures and the standard deviation capture the total risk of the investment, our results are shocking, that entropy gives almost twice as high an average explanatory power as the beta, with an average of 40% less standard deviation. A further contribution of the paper is that we introduce a simple method to estimate the entropy of a security or portfolio return.

Methodology ｜ 方法論 · 熵的思想譜系

¶6Entropy is a mathematically-defined quantity that is generally used for characterizing the probability of outcomes in a system that is undergoing a process. It was originally introduced in thermodynamics by Rudolf Clausius to measure the ratio of transferred heat through a reversible process in an isolated system. In statistical mechanics the interpretation of entropy is the measure of uncertainty about the system that remains after observing its macroscopic properties; this application was introduced by Ludwig Boltzmann, who defined the configuration entropy as the diversity of specific ways in which the components of the system may be arranged. There is an important application of entropy in information theory as well, and this is often called Shannon entropy. The entropy quantifies the expected value of the information in a message or, in other words, the amount of information that is missing before the message is received. The more unpredictable (uncertain) the message that is provided by the system, the greater the expected value of the information contained in the message. Consequently, greater uncertainty in the messages of the system means higher entropy. Because the entropy equals the amount of expected information in a message, it measures the maximum compression ratio that can be applied without losing information.

熵是一個數學上定義明確的量，用於刻畫過程中系統各種結果出現的機率。它最初由 Clausius 在熱力學中提出，衡量孤立系統可逆過程中的熱量傳遞比率；統計力學中，Boltzmann 將其詮釋為觀察到宏觀性質（壓力、溫度、體積）後系統仍殘餘的不確定性，並定義「組態熵」為系統組成元素可能排列方式的多樣性。熵在資訊理論中亦有關鍵應用，即 Shannon 熵：它量化訊息中資訊量的期望值，或說訊息被接收之前所缺失的資訊量。系統輸出的訊息愈難預測（愈不確定），訊息所含資訊的期望值就愈大——因此，不確定性愈高，熵愈高。由於熵等於訊息的期望資訊量，它同時度量了無損壓縮所能達到的最大壓縮率。

¶7In financial applications, Philippatos and Wilson find that entropy is more general and has some advantages over standard deviation in portfolio management. Kirchner and Zunckel argue that in financial economics entropy is a better tool for capturing the reduction of risk by diversification; however, in their study they suppose that the assets are Gaussian. Dionisio et al. argue that entropy observes the effect of diversification and is a more general measure of uncertainty than variance, since it uses more information about the probability distribution. Regarding the predictability of stock market returns, Maasoumi and Racine point out that entropy has several desirable properties and is capable of efficiently capturing nonlinear dependencies in return time series. Nawrocki and Harding propose applying state-value weighted entropy as a measure of investment risk; however, they are dealing with the discrete case. All the above academic papers recognize that entropy could be a good measure of risk; however, it seems to be difficult to use this measure. Our main motivation is to show that an entropy-based risk measure is, on the one hand, more precise, and, on the other hand, no more complicated to use than variance equilibrium models.

熵函數定義 ｜ 離散熵與微分熵

Discrete entropy function ｜ 離散熵函數

¶8Entropy functions can be divided into two main types, discrete and differential entropy functions. Let X* be a discrete random variable with outcomes o1,…,on and probabilities pi = Pr(X*=oi), pi ≥ 0, Σpi = 1. The generalized discrete entropy function (Rényi) for the variable X* is defined as:

Ha(X*) = 1⁄(1−a) · log(Σi=1..n pia)(1)

where a is the order of entropy (a ≥ 0, a ≠ 1), and the base of the logarithm is 2. The order of entropy expresses the weight taken into account in each outcome; if the order of entropy is lower, the more likely outcomes are underweighted, and vice versa. The most widely used orders are a=1 and a=2. The substitution of a=1 into (1) results in a division by zero; it can be shown, using l'Hôpital's rule, that Ha converges to the Shannon entropy:

H1(X*) = −Σi=1..n pi log(pi)(2)

The case of a=2 is called collision entropy, referred to as "Rényi entropy" further in the paper:

H2(X*) = −log(Σi=1..n pi2)(3)

Ha(X) is a non-increasing function in a, and both entropy measures are greater than zero provided that there is a finite number of possible outcomes: 0 < H2(X*) ≤ H1(X*) (4).

熵函數分為離散與微分（連續）兩大類。設 X* 為離散隨機變數，結果為 o₁,…,oₙ，對應機率 pᵢ。廣義離散熵（Rényi 族）如式 (1)，其中 a 為熵的階數（a ≥ 0 且 a ≠ 1），對數底為 2。階數決定各結果的權重：階數愈低，高機率結果被壓低權重的程度愈大，反之亦然。最常用的是 a=1 與 a=2。a=1 直接代入會除以零，但以 l'Hôpital 法則取極限可證其收斂至 Shannon 熵，即式 (2)；a=2 稱為碰撞熵，本文之後統稱 Rényi 熵，即式 (3)。Hₐ(X) 是 a 的非遞增函數，在有限結果數下兩種熵皆為正值，且恆有 0 < H₂ ≤ H₁，如式 (4)。
模型詳解 ①：Rényi 廣義熵族

統一框架：式 (1) 不是單一公式，而是一族以階數 a 為參數的熵。a 像一個「聚焦旋鈕」：a→0 時所有可能結果一視同仁（只數種類）；a=1 時按機率加權（Shannon）；a→∞ 時只看最大機率結果。

Shannon (a=1)：期望驚訝度，−Σp·log p。對分布尾部相對敏感。

Rényi/碰撞熵 (a=2)：−log Σp²。Σp² 是「隨機抽兩次落在同一格」的機率，故名碰撞熵；它放大高機率事件的權重，對尾部較不敏感——這解釋了後文為何 Rényi 熵長期解釋力最高（金融報酬中央峰態厚，主要資訊集中在高頻小報酬區）卻在短期樣本較不穩。

與你論文的連結你的 proposal 目前只用 Shannon 熵。本文提供現成理由，可在「未來延伸」中提及 a=2 作為穩健性檢驗，展示你知道熵是一個族而非單點。

Differential entropy function ｜ 微分熵函數

¶9Let X be a continuous random variable taking values from ℝ with a probability density function f(x). Analogously to (1), the continuous entropy is defined as:

Ha(X) = 1⁄(1−a) · ln ∫ f(x)a dx(5)

Although the entropy depends on the base of the logarithm, its value changes only by a constant coefficient for different bases; we use the natural logarithm for all differential entropy functions. The special cases are:

H1(X) = −∫ f(x) ln f(x) dx(6)
H2(X) = −ln ∫ f(x)² dx(7)

An important difference between discrete and continuous entropy is that while discrete entropy takes only non-negative values, continuous entropy can also take negative values: Ha(X) ∈ ℝ (8). In practice, standard risk measures like the CAPM beta or standard deviation are calculated on daily or monthly return data. Since the return on securities can take values from a continuous codomain, we primarily focus on the differential entropy function. However, by grouping return values into bins the discrete entropy function may also be used; this solution is outside the scope of this paper.

設 X 為取值於實數、具密度函數 f(x) 的連續隨機變數。仿照式 (1)，連續（微分）熵定義如式 (5)。熵值雖依對數底而異，但不同底之間僅差一常數係數；本文的微分熵一律採自然對數。特例 a=1、a=2 分別如式 (6)、(7)。離散熵與連續熵的一項關鍵差異：離散熵恆非負，連續熵卻可能為負，如式 (8)。實務上，貝塔與標準差等標準風險度量皆以日或月報酬計算；由於證券報酬取值於連續值域，本文以微分熵為主。當然，將報酬分組入 bin 後亦可使用離散熵，但該做法不在本文範圍內。
模型詳解 ②：微分熵及其陷阱

為何可為負：離散機率 pᵢ ≤ 1 故 −log pᵢ ≥ 0；密度 f(x) 卻可以 > 1（分布極窄時），使 −ln f 為負。日報酬的標準差常在 1–3%，密度峰值遠大於 1，因此金融日報酬的微分熵通常是負數——這正是後文式 (22) 要取指數變換的原因。

尺度不變性喪失：微分熵隨變數的線性縮放而平移（H(cX)=H(X)+ln|c|），意味著熵混入了波動幅度資訊——它與標準差的相關並非巧合，而是數學內建。

熵的估計模型 ｜ 直方圖 · 核密度 · 樣本間距

¶10For the estimation of differential entropy, the probability density function of the return values needs to be estimated. Let x1,…,xn be the observations of X, and Ha,n(X) the sample-based estimation of Ha(X). The plug-in estimations of entropy are calculated on the basis of the density function estimation; the probability density function f(x) is estimated by fn(x), the integral estimate of entropy, in the following way:

Ha,n(X) = 1⁄(1−a) · ln ∫An fn(x)a dx(9)

where An is the range of integration, which may exclude small and tail values of fn(x). We propose to select An = (min(x), max(x)).

¶11 · HistogramOne of the simplest methods of density estimation is the histogram-based density estimation. Let bn be the range of sample values; partition the range into k bins of equal width, with constant width h = bn/k. The density function is estimated as fn(x) = nj/(nh) if x falls in bin j, where nj is the number of data points in the jth bin (10). Based on the properties of the histogram, a simpler non plug-in estimation formula can be deduced for Shannon and Rényi entropy:

H1,n(X) = −1⁄n Σj=1..k nj ln( nj/(nh) )(11)
H2,n(X) = −ln Σj=1..k h·( nj/(nh) )²(12)

The parameter of this method is the number of equal width bins (k). However, there are several methods for choosing this parameter (e.g. the square root choice, Scott's normal reference rule, or the Freedman-Diaconis rule); the detailed descriptions of these are outside the scope of this paper.

【直方圖法】最簡單的密度估計即直方圖：將樣本範圍等寬切為 k 個 bin，每個 bin 寬度 h = bₙ/k，落在第 j 個 bin 內的密度估計為 nⱼ/(nh)，其中 nⱼ 為該 bin 的觀測筆數，如式 (10)。利用直方圖的性質，Shannon 與 Rényi 熵可推導出免積分的封閉解，分別如式 (11)、(12)——這正是摘要承諾的「簡便估計方法」。此法唯一的參數是 bin 數 k；選 k 的方法很多（平方根法、Scott 常態參考法則、Freedman–Diaconis 法則），惟其細節不在本文範圍內。
模型詳解 ③：直方圖熵估計（本文主力模型）

直覺：式 (11) 就是「離散 Shannon 熵＋寬度校正」。令 p̂ⱼ = nⱼ/n，可改寫為 H₁,ₙ = −Σ p̂ⱼ ln p̂ⱼ + ln h。前項是分箱後的離散熵，後項 ln h 把它校正回連續尺度。

這揭示了你已記錄的核心問題：+ln h 一項使估計值直接依賴 bin 寬，而等寬分箱下 h = (max−min)/k 由樣本極差決定——波動大 → 極差大 → h 大 → 熵機械性升高。本文的熵與標準差高度同步（Fig.1），很大程度是這個機制的產物，而非熵捕捉了獨立資訊。這是你「等寬分箱與波動度機械相關」批評的公式級證據，proposal 方法一節可直接引式 (11) 說明。

¶12 · KernelThe kernel-based density estimation is another commonly used method. It applies the formula fn(x) = (1/nh)·Σ K((x−xi)/h) (13), where K(·) is the kernel function and h is the bandwidth parameter. For practical reasons (computational time), we propose using the indicator-based Epanechnikov kernel function:

K(z) = ¾·(1−z²)·I{|z|≤1}(14)

Härdle shows that the choice of the kernel function is only of secondary importance, so the focus is rather on the right choice of bandwidth (h). One of the most widely used simple formulas for the estimation of h is Silverman's rule of thumb:

ĥrot = 1.06 · min{ s, IQR(x)/1.34 } · n−1/5(15)

As the formula assumes a normal distribution for X it gives an approximation for optimal bandwidth; despite this, Silverman's rule of thumb can be used for a good initial value for more sophisticated optimization methods.

【核密度法】核密度估計是另一常用方法：在每個觀測點放置一個以頻寬 h 縮放的核函數 K，再取平均，如式 (13)。基於計算效率考量，本文建議採用指示函數形式的 Epanechnikov 核，如式 (14)。Härdle 已證明核函數的選擇僅屬次要，真正關鍵在頻寬 h；最常用的簡便公式是 Silverman 經驗法則，如式 (15)，其中 IQR 為四分位距。此公式假設 X 服從常態分布，故僅為最適頻寬的近似——儘管如此，它仍可作為更精細最佳化方法的良好初始值。

¶13 · Sample spacingLet xn,1 ≤ xn,2 ≤ … ≤ xn,n be the ordered sample, assuming i.i.d. real-valued random variables. xn,i+m − xn,i is called a spacing of order m. The simple sample spacing density estimate is fn(x) = (m/n)·1/(xn,im − xn,(i−1)m) (16). Wachowiak et al. introduced another variation, called the Correa estimator (17), a locally regression-weighted refinement. The parameter for sample spacing methods is the fixed order m; for practical reasons we suggest using mn = ⌈n/k⌉, which depends on the size of the sample (18), where k is the number of bins. Beirlant et al. overview several additional entropy estimation methods, such as resubstitution, splitting-data and cross-validation; however, our paper focuses on the applications that are used most often.

【樣本間距法】將樣本排序後，相隔 m 位的兩個順序統計量之差稱為 m 階間距。最簡單的間距密度估計如式 (16)：資料愈密集處間距愈小、密度愈高。Wachowiak 等人提出的 Correa 估計量（式 17）是其局部迴歸加權的改良版。間距法的參數是階數 m；基於實務考量（樣本大小不一），本文建議取 mₙ = ⌈n/k⌉，使其隨樣本量調整，其中 k 為 bin 數，如式 (18)。Beirlant 等人回顧了再代入、分割資料、交叉驗證等更多熵估計方法，惟本文僅聚焦最常用者。

風險度量模型 ｜ 標準差 · CAPM 貝塔 · 熵基風險

¶14Let the following be a given set of data: D : {S, R, RM, RF} (19) — the set of securities S, the corresponding return observations R, the market return RM, and the risk-free return RF. In order to handle the risk measure uniformly, we introduce κ as a unified property for securities; let κ(Si) be the risk estimate for the security i. In the economic literature the most widely used risk measures are the standard deviation and the CAPM beta:

κ̂s(Si) = σ(Ri − RF)(20)
κ̂β(Si) = βi = cov(Ri−RF, RM−RF) / σ²(RM−RF)(21)

Our hypothesis is that uncertainty about the observation values can be interpreted as a risk of the security, and for this reason we apply entropy as a risk measure. Because the differential entropy function can also take negative values, for better interpretability we apply the exponential function to the entropy, and we define the entropy-based risk measure by the following formula:

κ̂H(Si) = eHn(Ri − RF)(22)

One can see that κH takes values from the non-negative real numbers, κH ∈ [0, +∞).

給定資料集 D：證券集合 S、對應報酬觀測 R、市場報酬 R_M 與無風險報酬 R_F。為統一處理各種風險度量，本文引入 κ 作為證券的統一風險屬性：κ(Sᵢ) 即證券 i 的風險估計值。文獻中最常用的兩種度量為標準差與 CAPM 貝塔，估計式分別如 (20)、(21)——注意兩者皆以「風險溢酬」（超額報酬）計算。本文的假設是：觀測值的不確定性可詮釋為證券的風險，因此以熵作為風險度量。由於微分熵可能為負值，為利於解讀，本文對熵取指數變換，定義熵基風險度量如式 (22)。如此 κ_H 恆為非負實數。

κ 框架的作用：把 σ、β、e^H 放進同一個函數介面，使「風險度量」成為可替換的模組——後文的 R² 比較因此在形式上公平。

Results ｜ 結果一 · 刻畫分散化效果

¶17We investigate whether entropy is able to measure the reduction of risk by diversification. We generate 10 million random equally-weighted portfolios with different numbers of securities involved (at most 100,000 for each size), based on the 150 randomly selected securities from the S&P500. The risk of portfolios is estimated by standard deviation, and by the Shannon and Rényi entropies using risk premiums for the full period. Because the CAPM beta measures the systematic risk only, we exclude it from the investigation of risk reduction. Both types of entropy functions are calculated by the histogram-based density function estimation, with 175 bins for the Shannon entropy and 50 bins for the Rényi entropy. (We tested the histogram, sample spacing and kernel density estimation methods, and the histogram-based method proved to be the most efficient in terms of explanatory and predictive power and simplicity.) For 10 random securities involved in the portfolio, approximately 40% of risk reduction can be achieved compared to a single random security, based on all of the three risk estimators under investigation.

結果二 · 長期解釋力

¶19In order to evaluate how efficiently the risk measures explain the expected risk premium over a long period, we estimate the risk for each security based on the full period (denoted by P1). Based on the equation of linear regressions, the average unexplained risk premium (intersect on the Y-axis or Jensen alpha) for the entropy methods (0.0091, 0.0059) is lower than that for the standard methods (0.0170 for standard deviation and 0.0209 for the CAPM beta).

Altogether, we argue that the test results for the full sample give a better comparison opportunity, as the sample sizes of the bullish and bearish markets are different and at the present moment the investor cannot decide whether there is an upward or a downward trend.

Conclusions ｜ 結論

¶24Entropy as a novel risk measure combines the advantages of the CAPM's risk parameter (beta) and the standard deviation. It captures risk without using any information about the market, and it is capable of measuring the risk reduction effect of diversification. The explanatory power for the expected return within the sample is better than the beta, especially in the long run covering bullish and bearish periods; the predictive power for the expected return is higher than for standard deviation. Both the Shannon and the Rényi entropies give more reliable risk estimation; their explanatory power exhibits significantly lower variance compared to the beta or the standard deviation. If upward and downward trends are distinguished, the regime dependency of entropy can be recognized: this result is similar to that for the beta. Among the entropy estimation methods reviewed, the histogram-based method proved to be the most efficient in terms of explanatory and predictive power; we propose a simple estimation formula for the Shannon and the Rényi entropy functions, which facilitates the application of an entropy-based risk measure.

作為新型風險度量，熵兼具 CAPM 貝塔與標準差兩者之長：它不需任何市場資訊即可捕捉風險，又能度量分散化的風險降低效果。樣本內對期望報酬的解釋力優於貝塔（尤以涵蓋牛熊的長期樣本為然）；樣本外預測力則高於標準差。Shannon 與 Rényi 熵皆提供更可靠的風險估計——其解釋力的變異顯著低於貝塔與標準差。若區分漲跌趨勢，熵的情境依賴性便會顯現，此性質與貝塔相仿。在所檢視的各種熵估計方法中，直方圖法在解釋力與預測力上最有效率；本文並提出 Shannon 與 Rényi 熵的簡便估計公式，使熵基風險度量的實務應用成為可能。

作者核心主張（一句話版本）

熵無需分布假設與市場資訊，即能以高於 CAPM 貝塔、且比貝塔與標準差都更穩定的解釋力為資產風險定價——代價僅是一條直方圖封閉解公式的計算量。
論證最強處「充分分散組合」檢驗（¶20）：作者主動走進對手主場——個別風險理應消失、β 最有利的情境——仍取得 1.5 倍解釋力優勢。先讓步機制（熵含個別風險）、再於讓步後的殘餘戰場獲勝，這種 concession–rebuttal 結構使證據幾乎無法被「熵只是混入了個別風險」一句話打發。
論證最弱處關鍵超參數（Shannon 175 bins／Rényi 50 bins）的選擇過程不透明（¶11, ¶17）：正文將 bin 數選擇「推給文獻」，實際數值卻在結果一節突然出現，且附表顯示係以表現擇優——若 k 是在同一資料上調出的，樣本內 R² 比較即含資料窺探，熵的優勢被高估的可能無法排除。
